HOW IT WORKS

From idea to flash in 6 steps

Every step runs in your browser. Nothing hidden, nothing proprietary.

01

Select Board

57 boards across Arduino, ESP, Raspberry Pi, STM32, micro:bit and Cytron Maker lines — with WiFi filter.

02

Add Components

Up to 5 from 127 indexed parts — sensors, displays, motors, relays, radios, all with real pin maps.

03

Validate

Voltage, conflicts, capabilities and power — flagged green/yellow/red/grey with fixes, never fake claims.

04

Route Pins

Conflict-free allocation with per-device SPI CS, input-only awareness — override any pin inline.

05

Generate Code

Clean C++ with your parameters, non-blocking millis(), syntax highlighted, one-click .ino or PlatformIO zip.

06

Flash & Monitor

Upload via your IDE or PlatformIO — then watch the output in the built-in Web Serial monitor.
